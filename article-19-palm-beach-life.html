Tag, You’re It

Dog tags have made the leap from soldier’s uniform to high fashion — with a spiritual twist. Celebrity jewelry designer Mary Margrill has accomplished this transcendence with her new Life Path Collection.

The diamond-encrusted tags feature numerology numbers, determined by adding together the numbers in your birth date until you reach a single character. Each number corresponds to a quality, such as power, kindness, or self-expression — your life path.

Denzel Washington and Johnny Depp already have their tags. Available in silver at The Rectory, Delray Beach (561-276-5833), or in gold by special order.
