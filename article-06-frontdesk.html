Cool = M2

Lindsay and Demi love these gems with a spiritual message

Usually it takes designers years and years of struggling to make a name for themselves. But Mary Margrill, the New York designer behind the M2 line (pronounced M-squared), found that celebrities and socialites were snatching up her jewels within just months of her launch. Because her unique collection is based on spirituality, everyone from Uma Thurman and Halle Berry quickly fell in love with the baubles that host positive messages, such as her popular pendants that say, “I am Blessed,” or “I am Loved.”

“I believe we can take a lot of strength from words,” Margrill says. “They inspire you, and remind you of what’s really important everyday.” But that doesn’t mean the pieces are hokey. “This isn’t a crunchy granola thing,” she says of the line. “It’s all solid 14 and 18kt gold, with diamonds and precious stones. And the words are about inspiring people and making a difference in their lives when they put the necklaces on each morning. It’s reminding themselves, and everyone of their belief or their daily mantra.” And it’s not just a cutesy gimmick Margrill came up with to sell her wares. “Everything I do in my own life is spiritual—from yoga to writing to meditating every morning,” she says. “It’s how I live, and I truly believe in infusing positive energy into each piece.”
